# BodyOS — Vision & Direction Document

**Purpose:** Reframe and expand the product vision beyond "posture correction" to its true identity: a body-aware performance intelligence system.

**For:** Development team continuing existing work

---

## 1. Vision Correction: What We Got Wrong

### Previous Framing (Incorrect)
> "FormFix is an AI-powered posture analysis app that identifies imbalances and corrects form."

This framing was **too narrow**. It positioned the product as:
- A problem-fixer
- A corrective tool
- A clinical utility

### True Vision
> "BodyOS is a personal body intelligence that sees you, remembers you, trains you, and evolves with you."

This is:
- A growth companion
- A performance enhancer
- A long-term ecosystem
- A coach that knows your body better than you do

## 5. Feature Ecosystem (Reframed)

These are not standalone features. They are **interconnected modules** that feed into a unified intelligence.

### Module 1: Body Intelligence (Formerly "Posture Analysis")

**What it does:**
- Captures standardized body photos (front/side/back)
- Analyzes skeletal alignment, imbalances, asymmetries
- Tracks changes over time with visual comparisons

**But more importantly:**
- This data FEEDS INTO everything else
- Workout plans account for imbalances
- Live coaching cues address YOUR specific body
- AI assistant references this in conversations

**Reframe:**
> Not "find what's wrong" but "understand your starting point and track your evolution."

### Module 2: Progress Capture

**What it does:**
- Regular progress photos (weekly/monthly)
- Side-by-side comparisons
- Visual timeline of transformation

**But more importantly:**
- AI can reference these: "Look how much your shoulders have improved since January"
- Motivational fuel during plateaus
- Objective evidence that transcends the scale

**Key insight:** Progress photos are not vanity — they're DATA. The AI uses them.

### Module 3: Live Movement Coach (Formerly "Live Form Analysis")

**What it does:**
- Real-time pose detection during exercises
- Visual skeleton overlay
- Rep counting
- Form scoring

**But more importantly — it's not about CORRECTION, it's about ENHANCEMENT:**

| Old Approach (Corrective) | New Approach (Performance) |
|---------------------------|----------------------------|
| "Your back is rounding" | "Brace your core — imagine someone's about to punch you" |
| "Knees are caving" | "Screw your feet into the ground — feel your glutes fire" |
| "Not deep enough" | "Break parallel, then DRIVE through your heels" |
| "Bad form" | "Good, now SQUEEZE at the top for max glute activation" |

**Performance cues the coach should give:**

For Squats:
- "Imagine spreading the floor apart with your feet"
- "Big breath, brace your core, then descend"
- "Drive your knees out over your toes"
- "Squeeze your glutes hard at lockout"

For Deadlifts:
- "Engage your lats — imagine bending the bar around your legs"
- "Push the floor away, don't pull the bar up"
- "Lock out with your glutes, not your lower back"

For Bench Press:
- "Imagine bending the bar into a U-shape — feel your lats engage"
- "Drive your feet into the floor"
- "Touch and EXPLODE"

For Rows:
- "Lead with your elbow, not your hand"
- "Squeeze your shoulder blade back at the top"
- "Control the negative — that's where growth happens"

**The AI should sound like an elite coach, not a medical device.**

### Module 4: Intelligent Workout Planning

**What it does:**
- Generates personalized workout plans
- Based on goals, equipment, schedule
- Includes exercises we have CV models for

**But more importantly:**
- Plans adapt based on body intelligence data
- If user has hip tilt → include corrective work automatically
- If user's squat form is improving → progress the load/complexity
- If user missed workouts → adjust the week

**Workout Plan Intelligence:**

```
User Goal: Build strength
User Equipment: Full gym
User Schedule: 4 days/week
User Body Data: Anterior pelvic tilt, tight hip flexors, right shoulder elevation

Generated Plan:
├── Day 1: Lower Body (Squat focus)

│   ├── Glute bridges (activation) — 2x15
│   ├── Squats (primary) — 4x6 [TRACKED]
│   ├── Romanian deadlift (posterior chain) — 3x10 [TRACKED]
│   └── Core: Dead bugs — 3x12 (reinforces pelvic stability)
│
├── Day 2: Upper Body (Push focus)
│   ├── Shoulder mobility (addresses elevation) — 2 min
│   ├── Bench press — 4x6 [TRACKED]
│   ├── Overhead press — 3x8 [TRACKED]
│   └── Tricep work — 3x12
│
... etc

[TRACKED] = These exercises have live CV coaching available
```

### Module 5: AI Assistant (The Brain)

**What it does:**
- Chat interface for questions, guidance, support
- Available in-app and via WhatsApp
- Generates plans, answers questions, tracks progress

**What it has access to:**
- All body scans and analysis
- All progress photos
- All workout history
- All form scores
- All conversation history
- User's goals, preferences, constraints

**This is the unifying intelligence.** It doesn't just answer questions — it KNOWS the user.

**Example interactions:**

User: "I'm feeling unmotivated today"
AI: "I get it. But look — you've been consistent for 8 weeks now. Your posture has measurably improved, and your squat form score went from 72 to 89. That's not nothing. Want to do a lighter session today? Sometimes showing up is the win."

User: "My shoulder hurts after bench press"
AI: "Let's think about this. Your right shoulder sits higher than your left — we've seen this in your scans. On bench, this can cause impingement if your setup isn't right. Let's review: are you retracting your shoulder blades fully before you press? Send me a video next session and I'll look specifically at this."

User: "Am I making progress?"
AI: [Shows progress photo comparison + posture improvement + strength progression graph]
"In 3 months: Your anterior tilt has reduced by ~40%. Your squat went from 60kg to 85kg with better form. Your shoulder alignment is improving. You're objectively better. The mirror lies sometimes — the data doesn't."

### Module 6: WhatsApp Coach

**What it does:**
- Same AI assistant, accessible via WhatsApp
- Quick questions without opening app
- Photo sharing for form checks
- Voice notes for hands-free

**Why it matters:**
- Meets user where they are
- Lower friction for small interactions
- Feels like texting your coach
- Accountability through casual check-ins

**Example flows:**

```
User (7am): "What should I eat before my workout?"
AI: "You've got lower body today — good carbs + moderate protein. 
     Oatmeal with banana, or rice with chicken. Eat 90 mins before. 
     How are you feeling about today's session?"

User (during workout): [sends video of squat]
AI: "Depth looks good! Watch your knees — they're drifting in slightly 
     on the way up. Think 'spread the floor' with your feet. 
     Also, your bracing is better than last week 💪"

User (10pm): "I'm craving junk food"
AI: "You've been disciplined for 6 days. One treat won't undo that. 
     But if you want to stay on track, try Greek yogurt with honey — 
     it usually kills the craving. Either way, you're good."
```

- A medical device ("Your lumbar lordosis is 34 degrees above normal")
- A nagging parent ("You should really fix your form")
- A generic chatbot ("I'm sorry, I don't understand")

**The AI is:**
- An elite coach who's worked with thousands of bodies
- Confident but not arrogant
- Direct but warm
- Knowledgeable but accessible
- Remembers everything, never judges

**Voice characteristics:**

| Attribute | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| Confident | "Your right shoulder is definitely sitting higher. We'll address it." |
| Encouraging | "This is fixable. Most people with desk jobs have this." |
| Direct | "Your squat depth isn't there yet. Let's work on ankle mobility." |
| Performance-focused | "Squeeze your glutes at the top — that's free gains." |
| Contextual | "Given your hip tilt, focus extra on bracing during deadlifts." |
| Memorable | "Remember last month when you couldn't hit parallel? Look at you now." |

**The AI should feel like that coach who:**
- Remembers your name
- Remembers your injury from 6 months ago
- Gives you specific cues that work for YOUR body
- Celebrates your wins
- Pushes you when you're slacking
- Never makes you feel stupid for asking questions

### Visual Texture

#### Soft Noise Background

Apply a subtle noise texture to backgrounds to:
- Add depth and tactility
- Prevent flat, digital feeling
- Create premium, engineered aesthetic

**Specification:**
- Noise type: Monochromatic grain
- Opacity: 3-5%
- Blend mode: Overlay or Soft Light
- Scale: Fine grain (not visible as individual pixels)

```css
/* Example implementation */
.background {
  background-color: #0E0F12;
  background-image: url('noise-texture.png');
  background-blend-mode: overlay;
  opacity: 0.03;
}
```

#### Subtle Gradients

Use gradients to create depth, not decoration:

```css
/* Card elevation gradient */
.card {
  background: linear-gradient(
    180deg,
    #15171C 0%,
    #0E0F12 100%
  );
}

/* Blueprint glow behind key elements */
.highlight-glow {
  background: radial-gradient(
    ellipse at center,

  );
```

### Live Analysis Overlay Design

The skeleton overlay during live coaching must feel:
- **Precise** — Clean lines, exact positioning
- **Informative** — Color communicates state
- **Non-intrusive** — Enhances video, doesn't obscure

#### Skeleton Rendering

```
     ●────●  (shoulders)           ● = Joint marker (6px circle)
       │                           ─ = Bone connection (2px line)
     ●─┼─●  (hips)
      /│\
     ● │ ●  (knees)
      \│/
     ●─┴─●  (ankles)
```

**Joint markers:**
- Size: 6px diameter circles
- Color: Blueprint blue by default
- Transition to cyan (good) or amber (attention) based on form

**Bone connections:**
- Width: 2px lines
- Color: Blueprint blue at 60% opacity
- Smooth bezier curves, not harsh straight lines

**Form feedback zones:**
- Subtle glow around joints needing attention
- Pulsing animation (slow, 2s cycle) for active corrections

### Animation Principles

#### Philosophy

Animations should feel:
- **Purposeful** — Every animation communicates something
- **Quick** — 150-300ms for most transitions
- **Subtle** — Enhance, don't distract
- **Consistent** — Same easing curves throughout

#### Timing

| Animation Type | Duration | Easing |
|----------------|----------|--------|
| Button press | 100ms | ease-out |
| Card appearance | 200ms | ease-out |
| Modal open | 250ms | ease-out |
| Modal close | 200ms | ease-in |
| Page transition | 300ms | ease-in-out |
| Skeleton tracking | Real-time | linear |
| Form feedback pulse | 2000ms | ease-in-out (loop) |

#### Skeleton Overlay Animation

- Landmark positions: Interpolate smoothly between frames
- No jitter — use temporal smoothing
- Form state changes: 300ms color transition
- Rep completion: Brief scale pulse (1.0 → 1.1 → 1.0, 200ms)

### Key Data Relationships

#### 1. Posture → Training Integration

When a user completes a posture scan, the detected issues flow into workout planning:

```sql
-- User has anterior pelvic tilt detected
posture_assessments.detailed_issues = [
  {"issue": "anterior_pelvic_tilt", "severity": 0.7}
]

-- Workout plan automatically includes corrective work
workout_plan_exercises.personalized_notes = 
  "Focus on bracing due to your hip tilt"

-- Live coaching references the same data
coaching_cues.personalized_cues.anterior_pelvic_tilt = 
  "Brace your core extra hard — this protects your lower back"
```

Every workout session enriches the AI's understanding:

```sql

```

```
   └── auth.users created (Supabase Auth)

   └── profiles.equipment set
   └── profiles.onboarding_completed = true

   └── posture_assessments created
   └── activity_feed entry created (trigger)
   └── achievement checked: 'first-scan'
```

```
4. AI generates first workout plan
   └── workout_plans created
   └── workout_plan_days created (3-4 days)

5. User completes first session
   └── exercise_sessions created
   └── session_sets created (per set)
   └── session_reps created (per rep, if CV tracked)
   └── activity_feed entry (trigger)
   └── user_streaks.current_streak = 1
   └── achievement checked: 'first-session'

6. User asks AI a question
   └── ai_conversations created
   └── ai_messages created (user + assistant)
   └── achievement checked: 'ai-conversation'
```

```
7. User takes progress photo

   └── activity_feed entry

   └── comparison_to_previous calculated
   └── If improved: achievement checked 'posture-improved'

9. User hits 7-day streak
   └── user_streaks.current_streak = 7
```
