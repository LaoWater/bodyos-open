# Contribute to BodyOS

Help make this useful for people who train and people who coach them. Documentation, accessibility, exercise knowledge, translations and small fixes are welcome alongside code.

1. Check existing issues. For a larger change, open one explaining who it helps and what would change.
2. Fork the repository, create a branch, and read the README for the part you will touch.
3. Keep the change focused. Use invented or consented sample data; never include client records, credentials or private recordings.
4. In your pull request, explain the change and how you inspected it. Include before/after pictures for a visual change and identify checks you did not run. There is no requirement to install the whole stack.

Use respectful, practical discussion. Maintainers may close abusive or unrelated submissions. A contribution you intentionally submit is offered under the [BodyOS Community License](LICENSE). Confirm you have the rights to submit it; identify third-party material and retain its notices. No transfer of your copyright is required.

For security issues, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting when available; do not put working exploits, access tokens or personal data in a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
